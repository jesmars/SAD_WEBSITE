How your estimate is computed

Staff encode your site measurements and gate specifications. The system lists the materials needed and computes a baseline cost from the price list kept by the owner. The owner reviews and approves the estimate before it reaches you as a quotation.

Downpayments and final payments are made offline (cash or bank transfer) and verified by staff.
