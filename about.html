Our story

From a marble workshop to a full fabrication shop

A&N Construction and Marble Works began in the 1980s as a small local enterprise focused on marble craftsmanship, masonry and stone fabrication. As client demand grew to larger structural and architectural projects, the business broadened its services into general construction and metal fabrication.

The current owner learned the trade in his youth by helping his father in the marble workshop, and continues to run the business today.
